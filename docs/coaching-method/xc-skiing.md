# Cross-country skiing (tek na smučeh)

Part of the [coaching method](../coaching-method.md). Source numbers refer to its section 12.

**What decides performance:** very high VO₂max, upper-body endurance and power (double poling), technique in both classic and skating, and the ability to change pace on climbs. Elite skiers train 750–950 h a year, about 90 % of sessions at low intensity, with a large share on roller skis in summer [32].

## Season
| Months | Phase | Main training |
|---|---|---|
| Apr | transition | rest, other sports |
| May–Aug | base | roller skis (classic and skating), running, ski walking with poles, strength |
| Sep–Oct | build | roller ski intervals, uphill bounding with poles, double poling |
| Nov | build on snow | first snow (glacier, artificial loops), technique |
| Dec–Mar | races | race-specific sessions, taper per race (section 7) |

Recreational athletes often aim at one or two long races (e.g. a ski marathon): their build peaks in January–February.

## Key sessions
Lead metric: HR (and RPE for short intervals). Snow, wax and terrain change speed too much for pace targets.

| Session | Prescription | Zone | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long easy ski or roller ski | 1.5–3 h, mixed classic/skating | Z1–Z2 | aerobic base, technique volume |
| Threshold intervals | 4–5 × 8 min on rolling terrain, 2 min easy between | Z4 | race pace |
| VO₂max intervals uphill | 5–8 × 3–4 min skating or diagonal stride uphill, easy back down | Z5 | VO₂max, climbing |
| Double poling intervals | 6–8 × 3 min double poling on flat to slight uphill, 2 min easy | Z4 | upper-body endurance |
| Uphill bounding with poles (summer) | 6–10 × 2–3 min on a steep trail, walk down | Z4–Z5 | ski-specific power and VO₂max |
| Speed | 6–10 × 15 s sprints, full recovery | neuromuscular | speed, technique at race pace |

## Strength and technique
- **Strength** (2× a week May–Oct, 1× in season): pull-downs and triceps work for poling, squats, single-leg work for push-off, core (anti-rotation). 3–4 sets of 4–8 reps, heavy [31].
- **Upper-body endurance:** double poling on roller skis or a ski ergometer, 1–2× a week in base.
- **Technique** in every easy session: one drill block of 10–15 min (no-pole skating for balance, one-skate for timing, double poling without legs). Film the athlete twice a season.
- Roller ski safety: helmet, quiet roads or roller ski tracks, speed reducers on downhills for beginners (convention).

## Specific risks and rules
- Cold air and hard intervals can trigger exercise-induced bronchoconstriction: hard sessions below about −15 °C are moved indoors or made easy (convention).
- Double poling volume rises like running volume: count it in the weekly limit and increase gradually; elbow and shoulder overuse is common.
