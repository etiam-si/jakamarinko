# Road cycling (cestno kolesarstvo)

Part of the [coaching method](../coaching-method.md). Source numbers refer to its section 12.

**What decides performance:** power at LT2 (FTP) relative to body mass on climbs, absolute power on flats, repeated hard efforts, and long-ride endurance and fuelling. Cycling has no impact, so it tolerates more volume than running; athletes ride 1.5–3× the hours they would run.

## Season
| Months | Phase | Main training |
|---|---|---|
| Oct–Nov | transition | rest, other sports, gym |
| Dec–Feb | base | indoor trainer and easy outdoor rides, strength |
| Mar–Apr | build | longer rides, threshold, climbs |
| Apr–Sep | races and gran fondos | race-specific sessions, taper per race |

## Key sessions
Lead metric: power (W). HR when there is no power meter; HR zones from a running test are shifted by −7 bpm (method section 2.5).

| Session | Prescription | Zone | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long ride | 2–5 h steady, cadence 80–95 | Z1–Z2 | aerobic base, fat use, fuelling practice |
| Threshold | 2 × 20 min or 3 × 12–15 min, 5 min easy between | Z4 (95–103 % of LT2 power) | FTP |
| Tempo (sweet spot) | 2–3 × 15–20 min at the top of Z3 | Z3 | long-race pace, time-efficient |
| VO₂max | 5 × 4–5 min at 106–120 % of FTP, equal recovery; or 3 sets of 10 × 30/30 s | Z5 | VO₂max |
| Climbing repeats | 4–6 × 6–10 min on a climb | Z4 | climbing, pacing |
| Sprints | 6–8 × 10–15 s, full recovery, in an easy ride | neuromuscular | sprint, race surges |
| Low cadence | 4–6 × 5 min at 55–65 rpm on a climb | Z3 | force, seated climbing |

Power zones follow the athlete's LT1/LT2 power or FTP; the classic power-zone system [10] is a reference for coaches.

## Strength and technique
- **Strength** (2× a week in transition and base, 1× from build): squats, leg press, single-leg work, deadlifts, core and back. Heavy, 3–4 sets of 4–8 reps; it improves cycling economy and sprint power [31].
- Cyclists who only ride lose bone density: one session a week of jumping or running is worth keeping for bone health (convention, related to [28]).
- **Technique:** group riding and cornering, descending; bike fit before raising volume (refer out, not part of the plan).

## Specific risks and rules
- Traffic safety: intervals on quiet roads or the trainer, never in fast traffic (convention).
- Long rides need fuelling: the plan reminds to eat and drink on rides over 90 min.
- Indoor sessions are shorter for the same effect: 60–75 min indoors is a full session (convention).
