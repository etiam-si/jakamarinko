# Mountain running (gorski tek)

Part of the [coaching method](../coaching-method.md). Source numbers refer to its section 12.

**What decides performance:** VO₂max and threshold on climbs, running economy uphill, power hiking with or without poles on steep ground, and eccentric strength for downhills. Uphill running costs more energy and loads the calves; downhill running costs less energy but causes much more muscle damage [34].

## Season
| Months | Phase | Main training |
|---|---|---|
| Nov | transition | rest, other sports |
| Dec–Mar | base | easy running, ski touring or XC skiing for volume, strength |
| Apr–May | build | hill intervals, first mountain runs as snow melts |
| May–Oct | races | race-specific sessions, taper per race |

Race types vary a lot: vertical kilometre (30–60 min, all uphill), uphill races, classic mountain races (up and down, 1–2 h), and long trail races (3 h+). The plan follows the A race's profile.

## Key sessions
Lead metric: HR and vertical speed (m/h) uphill; pace only on flat sections.

| Session | Prescription | Zone | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long mountain run | 1.5–4 h with steady climbing, run or hike by gradient | Z1–Z2 | aerobic base, time on feet, downhill tolerance |
| Hill repeats | 6–10 × 2–4 min uphill, jog or walk down | Z5 | VO₂max, uphill power |
| Threshold climb | 2–3 × 12–15 min uphill, 3–4 min easy between | Z4 | VK and uphill race pace |
| Tempo with vert | 40–60 min including long climbs at steady effort | Z3 | long races |
| Power hiking with poles | 4–6 × 6–8 min on 25 %+ gradient | Z3–Z4 | steep race sections, VK |
| Downhill practice | 4–6 × 2–3 min controlled fast downhill, walk up | Z2 + skill | technique, eccentric tolerance |
| Strides | 4–6 × 20 s fast on flat or slight uphill after an easy run | — | form, economy |

- **Jaka's benchmark:** a yearly Šmarna gora climb (from Tacen) works as a field test and a progress marker for any athlete in Ljubljana.

## Strength and technique
- **Strength** (2× a week in base, 1× in season): split squats, step-ups, single-leg deadlifts, calf raises (straight and bent knee), eccentric step-downs for downhills, hip and core work. Heavy, 3–4 sets of 4–8 reps [31].
- **Plyometrics** (1× a week in base and build): skipping, bounding uphill, single-leg hops; low volume, progressive.
- **Technique:** short quick steps uphill, when to switch to hiking (when hiking is as fast as running at the same HR), downhill: look ahead, quick feet, relaxed arms.

## Specific risks and rules
- Downhill volume is the main source of soreness and injury: increase total descent per week by no more than about 10 % (convention, following the volume rules).
- Running volume counts toward HR-02 in full, including hiking sections of a run.
- Mountain weather: the plan says when a session should be moved below the treeline in storms (convention).
