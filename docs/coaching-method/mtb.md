# Mountain biking (gorsko kolesarstvo)

Part of the [coaching method](../coaching-method.md). Source numbers refer to its section 12.

**What decides performance:** in cross-country (XCO, 60–90 min), repeated short efforts well above LT2 on steep climbs, VO₂max and technical skill; in marathons (XCM, 3 h+), threshold and long-ride endurance plus fuelling. Riding skill saves more energy and time than most fitness gains on technical courses.

## Season
| Months | Phase | Main training |
|---|---|---|
| Oct–Nov | transition | rest, other sports, gym |
| Dec–Feb | base | road bike or trainer, cross-country skiing, strength, skills on dry days |
| Mar–Apr | build | climbs, short intervals, skills |
| Apr–Oct | races | race-specific sessions, taper per race |

## Key sessions
Lead metric: HR, or power if a meter is fitted. RPE on technical terrain, where HR and power jump around.

| Session | Prescription | Zone | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long ride on trails | 2–4 h, mostly steady, technical sections easy | Z1–Z2 | aerobic base, skills under fatigue |
| Short-short intervals | 2–3 sets of 10–13 × 30 s hard / 15–30 s easy | Z5 | XCO race pattern |
| VO₂max climbs | 5–6 × 3–4 min on a climb, ride down easy | Z5 | VO₂max, climbing |
| Threshold climbs | 3–4 × 8–12 min | Z4 | XCM pace, sustained climbs |
| Start efforts | 4–6 × 1 min all-out from standing, then 2 min at Z4 | Z5 | XCO start |
| Skills session | 45–60 min drills: corners, switchbacks, roots, drops, braking | Z1–Z2 | technique |

Road sessions from [road-cycling.md](road-cycling.md) work for base and threshold.

## Strength and technique
- **Strength** (2× a week in base, 1× in season): squats, deadlifts, single-leg work, upper body and grip (pull-ups, rows, push-ups), core. Heavy, 3–4 sets of 4–8 reps [31].
- **Technique:** one skills block a week all season. Line choice, body position on descents, cornering, track stands, climbing steep technical sections seated vs standing.

## Specific risks and rules
- Falls are common: any blow to the head is RF-03; a helmet in every session.
- New technical skills are practised on easy days, not at the end of a hard session (convention).
- Skills sessions count as endurance time in Z1–Z2 for the weekly volume.
