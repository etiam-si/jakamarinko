# Ski touring (turno smučanje)

Part of the [coaching method](../coaching-method.md). Source numbers refer to its section 12.

**What decides performance:** VO₂max and threshold on long climbs, efficiency per kg (athlete + kit), fast transitions, and safe, quick descents. Races are mostly uphill at Z3–Z4, with short Z5 sections; vertical race formats are one long Z4 effort [33].

## Season
| Months | Phase | Main training |
|---|---|---|
| May | transition | rest, hiking, cycling |
| Jun–Sep | base | uphill running and hiking with poles, roller skis, cycling, strength |
| Oct–Nov | late base, early build | uphill intervals with poles, first days on snow (glacier or early snow) |
| Dec–Jan | build | on skis: intervals, long tours, transitions |
| Jan–Mar | races | race-specific sessions, taper per race (section 7) |
| Apr | late season | long tours, then transition |

## Key sessions
Lead metric: HR and vertical speed (m/h) on a known climb. Downhills are recovery in zone terms but count in duration.

| Session | Prescription | Zone | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long tour | 2–5 h, mostly uphill at steady effort, descents included | Z1–Z2 | aerobic base, fat use, time on skis |
| Threshold climbs | 3–4 × 10–12 min uphill, 3 min easy between | Z4 | race pace for individual races |
| VO₂max climbs | 5–6 × 4 min uphill, 3 min easy descent or walk between | Z5 | VO₂max, steep race sections |
| Tempo climb | 30–45 min continuous uphill | Z3 | long races, pacing |
| Transition drill | 6–10 × (3 min hard climb + skins off + 1 min descent + skins on) | Z4 + skill | race transitions under fatigue |
| Vertical race simulation | 20–40 min all-out uphill (also a field test) | Z4 | vertical races; retest of LT2 HR and m/h |

**Summer equivalents:** uphill running or fast hiking with poles on steep trails replaces skinning (same muscles, similar HR); roller skis for poling; long hikes for time on feet.

## Strength and technique
- **Strength** (2× a week in base, 1× in season): squats or split squats, step-ups with load, deadlifts, calf raises, core and upper back for poling. 3–4 sets of 4–8 reps, heavy and controlled, not to failure; heavy strength improves economy in endurance athletes [31].
- **Plyometrics** (base only, 1× a week): box jumps, bounding uphill, 4–6 × 6–8 contacts.
- **Technique:** kick turns on steep terrain, efficient stride (glide the ski, don't lift it), pole plant rhythm, transitions (target under 30 s at race level), descending in variable snow with a light race kit.

## Specific risks and rules
- Avalanche safety is outside the plan: the plan never chooses terrain. Every on-snow session says "choose a safe route for today's avalanche conditions" (convention).
- Cold and altitude raise HR; long tours follow RPE when HR reads high.
- Descents with tired legs cause most injuries: the last descent of a hard session is taken easy.
