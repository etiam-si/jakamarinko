## About me
I'm Jaka Marinko, an endurance athlete, a student at the Faculty of Sport and a personal trainer who specialises in preparing athletes for endurance sports.

Sport has been part of my everyday life since I was a child. Years of racing at the top junior level gave me experience that I now combine with current sports science, to help others improve faster and more safely.

As a multiple junior national champion in ski mountaineering, cross-country skiing, roller skiing and mountain running, I've learned what it takes to reach top results.

My measured VO₂max is 80 ml/kg/min.

I'm especially proud of 5th place at Red Bull 400 Planica, where I set the fastest time among the juniors.

Today I build on that practical experience with my studies at the Faculty of Sport, going deeper into physiology, biomechanics, training methodology and sport psychology.

I believe the best results don't happen by chance. They come from careful planning, consistency and an individual approach.

## Coaching
**Training built around you.** Science, experience and consistent tracking of your progress, combined in one system.

| Service | Description |
|---|---|
| Individual coaching | A plan fitted to your goal, your daily routine and your current fitness. |
| Online coaching | Weekly planning, adjustments along the way and ongoing contact, wherever you live. |
| Race preparation | Season periodisation, peaking at the right time and tactics for your key races. |
| Watch data analysis | Interpreting the training load, heart rate and recovery data from your Garmin or other sports watch. |
| Progress review | A look back at your training and the long-term trends in your progress. |
| Mental preparation | Working on motivation, focus and handling race pressure. |
| Biomechanics | Analysis of your technique and movement patterns, for more efficiency and fewer injuries. |
| Physiology | Training zones, the aerobic threshold and endurance development on a scientific basis. |

The old site's "Garmin analysis" and "Strava analysis" are renamed here: we don't use the Strava API (see `docs/product.md`), and analysis covers any watch, not only Garmin. Strava segments are left out for the same reason.
